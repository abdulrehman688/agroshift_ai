Suggested wording for updating the signed course proposal. The original PDF has not been changed.

## Fertilizer and pesticide wording

**Original meaning:** the app gives no fertilizer or pesticide doses and always advises checking with a local expert.

**Suggested replacement:**

> AgroShift does not generate, calculate or recommend fertilizer or pesticide doses. A future guidance library may display amounts exactly as published by official agricultural sources, with the source and date, after human review. Farmers are always advised to follow the product label and check with a local agriculture officer.

The current app shows no dose library.

## Expanded scope

**Suggested addition:**

> With the course teacher's encouragement, the project expands beyond the original rotation planner into a multi-screen farm companion, including multi-farm management, irrigation estimates, canal turn reminders, weather alerts, farm records, sales tracking, an interactive crop guide and a field-aware multilingual assistant.
