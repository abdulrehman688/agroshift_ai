# Field Lens

Back to [[Home]]. **Status: planned.** Full rules are in section 13 of [[PROJECT_CONTEXT]].

The farmer takes a crop photo, asks by voice or text, and gets an answer tied to that field.

## Evidence

1. The photo: visible symptoms
2. The field: crop, stage and history
3. NASA data: rain, heat, moisture, vegetation and water stress
4. Official guidance: reviewed Pakistani sources

## Rules

- NASA data shows field conditions. It cannot identify a disease from a phone photo.
- Every fact must link to stored evidence.
- If evidence is missing, the answer says so.
- AI never generates or calculates a fertilizer or pesticide dose.
- A cause is confirmed only by an expert or suitable test.
